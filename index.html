More Than Bookkeeping

Recording the Past Is the Starting Line, Not the Finish

Good bookkeeping is essential. It is also, on its own, a rear-view mirror. It tells you what the business did last month with no opinion about what you should do next.

The value sits in the layer above it. Which products actually carry your margin. What happens to cash if revenue softens 20 percent. Whether this is the quarter to hire, or the quarter to hold. Those are planning questions, and they need a forward model rather than a historical report.

That is the difference between a firm that categorizes transactions and a partner who helps you make better decisions. Read more on when bookkeeping stops being enough.

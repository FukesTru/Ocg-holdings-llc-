The Overview

The Expensive Version of Taxes Is the Rushed Version

When records get rebuilt in March, two things happen: deductions get missed, and decisions that could have saved real money — timing purchases, structuring compensation, planning distributions — happened months ago without anyone running the tax math.

OCG flips the calendar. Your books stay reconciled and tax-ready every month through our bookkeeping service. Planning conversations happen before year-end, while there's still time to act. Filing season becomes a checkpoint on work that's already done.

Already have a CPA you like? Keep them. We make their job easier — clean, organized, tax-ready records are the best gift a preparer can receive. Or we coordinate the tax function directly, depending on your engagement.

Questions

Tax Support, Explained

Do you replace my CPA?

Not necessarily. We can support your existing CPA with clean, organized, tax-ready records — or coordinate the tax function directly, depending on your engagement.

What does "year-round" tax support actually mean?

Your records stay organized and reconciled all year, planning conversations happen before year-end while there's still time to act, and deadlines are tracked continuously — instead of everything happening in a March scramble.

Can you help if I'm behind on taxes?

Yes. We start by rebuilding clean books for the affected periods — the foundation any catch-up filing needs — and coordinate the path to current compliance.

Will this actually save me money?

Clean records and proactive planning mean deductions don't get missed and decisions with tax consequences get made deliberately. Most owners overpay when their books are a mess — it's one of the four problems on our homepage for a reason.

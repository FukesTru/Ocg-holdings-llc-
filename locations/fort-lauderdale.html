The Local Picture

Built for How Fort Lauderdale Does Business

Broward businesses often grow in the shadow of Miami's noise — which suits the operators just fine. What doesn't suit them is financial infrastructure that lags the business: books weeks behind, margins nobody can state precisely, and cash decisions made on the balance shown in the banking app.

OCG replaces that with a system: a monthly close you can set your watch to, forward-looking cash visibility, and financials that inspire confidence — whether the audience is you, your partners, or the lender considering your next stage of growth.

OCG Financial is founder-led by Oscar Cancino — an operator with a private equity background who's built, bought, and sold businesses. That experience, not a textbook, shapes every engagement.

Historical Cleanup

Months (or Years) Behind? That's Where We Usually Start.

Most clients don't arrive with pristine books. They arrive with a backlog: uncategorized transactions, unreconciled accounts, a chart of accounts that grew like a weed, and a nagging sense that the numbers can't be trusted.

Cleanup is a defined project, not an open meter. We scope it in the readiness assessment, rebuild the affected periods, reconcile everything, and hand you a clean baseline — then keep it clean with the monthly service.

It's also the fastest-payback work we do: clean books immediately unlock accurate taxes, honest margins, and credible funding conversations.

The Cleanup Sequence
1. Scope the backlog & fix the chart of accountsDays
2. Rebuild & categorize affected periodsWks 1–3
3. Reconcile every account to the statementWks 2–4
4. Deliver clean baseline financialsWk 4

Questions

Bookkeeping, Explained

My books are months behind. Can you fix that?

Yes. Historical cleanup is one of the most common ways engagements start. We rebuild your records, reconcile every account, and hand you a clean, current set of books.

What software do you work with?

Modern cloud accounting stacks — QuickBooks Online and the tools that connect to it, including eCommerce platforms, payment processors, and inventory systems.

How fast is the monthly close?

Once onboarded, most clients receive reconciled financials and reporting within the first half of the following month — consistently, every month.

Is bookkeeping enough, or do I need a CFO too?

Bookkeeping keeps your records right; CFO strategy uses them to drive decisions. Many clients start with bookkeeping and layer in fractional CFO support as they grow — read our guide on when that moment arrives.

The Local Picture

Built for How Houston Does Business

Cyclical and capital-intensive businesses live or die on two things: how much cash is tied up in operations, and how well they can see the next twelve months. Get either wrong and a good year funds a bad one you didn't forecast.

OCG's Houston work focuses there: scenario planning that models the downside honestly, working-capital discipline that frees trapped cash, and lender-ready reporting so credit is available before you need it rather than after.

OCG Financial is founder-led by Oscar Cancino — an operator with a private equity background who's built, bought, and sold businesses. We work remote-first with clients across the country, which means Houston businesses get senior attention without paying for local overhead.

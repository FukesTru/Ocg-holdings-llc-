Three Things Worth Knowing

The Advice That Changes the Most Outcomes

Nothing complicated. These are simply the ideas that separate owners who feel in control of their numbers from those who do not.

Profit on Paper Is Not Cash in the Bank

A strong profit and loss statement can sit alongside a genuine cash problem. Revenue is earned on one schedule and collected on another, and growth widens that gap before it closes it. Track cash separately, always.

Late Numbers Are Almost as Costly as Wrong Ones

Financials that arrive six weeks after month end cannot inform a decision you already made. A monthly close on a predictable schedule is worth more than a perfect one that comes too late to use.

Systems Make a Business Worth More

A company that runs on process, documentation, and accountability is worth more than one that runs on the owner's memory. Buyers, lenders, and partners all pay for that difference.

Understand Your Own Numbers

Outsourcing the work is sensible. Outsourcing the understanding is not. The owners who make the best decisions are the ones who can read their own financials and ask sharper questions because of it.

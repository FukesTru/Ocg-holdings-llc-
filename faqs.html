Working With OCG

What does OCG Financial actually do?

We're a founder-led financial partner for growing businesses. We combine bookkeeping, fractional CFO strategy, year-round tax support, and business funding readiness into one integrated system — so your books, forecasts, taxes, and capital plans all work together instead of living with four different vendors.

How is OCG different from a traditional accounting firm?

Most firms are built by accountants. OCG was built by an operator — founder Oscar Cancino has invested in, acquired, operated, and sold businesses across retail, e-commerce, and real estate. We focus on forward-looking decisions (cash flow, margins, capital) rather than only recording what already happened. Read the full story.

What size of business do you work with?

Our clients are typically founder-led businesses that have outgrown DIY bookkeeping but don't need a full-time CFO — including more than 30 eCommerce brands. If your numbers have started driving real decisions (hiring, inventory, funding), you're in the right range. See the industries we serve.

Do you work with businesses outside Florida?

Yes — OCG is remote-first and works with founder-led businesses nationwide. We have dedicated coverage in New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Dallas, Houston, Atlanta, Austin, and Denver, plus our Florida home market (Miami, Tampa, Orlando, Jacksonville, Fort Lauderdale, and West Palm Beach). Every engagement runs the same way regardless of city. See all service locations.

Onboarding & Day to Day

How quickly can we get started?

Onboarding typically starts within days of the consultation. Our fractional CFO onboarding gets reporting, forecasting, and cash-flow visibility in place early in the engagement.

My books are a mess. Is that a problem?

It's the most common starting point. Historical cleanup is a core part of our bookkeeping service — we rebuild your records, reconcile every account, and hand you a clean, current foundation before layering strategy on top.

What software do you work with?

Modern cloud accounting stacks — QuickBooks Online and the ecosystem around it, including eCommerce platforms, payment processors, and inventory tools. If your stack is unusual, we'll flag it in the assessment.

Who will I actually be working with?

OCG is founder-led. Strategy conversations happen with people who carry real accountability for your outcome — not a rotating cast of junior staff.

Tax & Funding

Do you replace my CPA or tax preparer?

Not necessarily. Our tax support keeps your books clean, current, and tax-ready year-round. We can work alongside your existing CPA or coordinate the tax function directly, depending on the engagement.

Are you a lender?

No — we make you fundable. Our business funding service builds lender-grade financials, shapes your capital strategy, and supports you through the raise. OCG has supported $35M+ in capital raises.

When should I start preparing to raise capital?

Before you need the money. Lenders fund businesses whose numbers they trust, and that trust — clean books, consistent reporting, a credible forecast — takes months to build, not days. Read our guide to cash flow mistakes for the usual warning signs.

The Overview

Your Books Tell You What Happened. A CFO Tells You What to Do.

Most growing businesses have the recording layer — someone keeps the books, someone files the taxes. What's missing is the decision layer: who reads those numbers, models what's coming, and turns them into pricing, hiring, inventory, and capital moves.

That's the layer OCG's CFO service installs. We build your reporting and forecasting infrastructure, then run a standing cadence where real decisions get made against real numbers — before the month ends, not after.

It sits on top of clean books (we handle cleanup and monthly bookkeeping if yours need work) and connects directly to capital readiness when funding enters the picture.

Questions

CFO Services, Explained

What do CFO services include?

Cash flow forecasting, margin and unit-economics analysis, budgeting, KPI dashboards, board and investor reporting, and standing strategic guidance — the full financial leadership layer of your business.

How is this different from my accountant or bookkeeper?

Bookkeepers and accountants record and report what already happened. A CFO uses those numbers to shape what happens next: pricing, hiring, inventory, funding, and growth decisions.

How long does onboarding take?

Our structured onboarding gets core reporting, forecasting, and cash-flow visibility in place within the first 30 days.

Do I need clean books first?

No — if your books need work, we handle cleanup as part of onboarding through our bookkeeping service, so your CFO layer is built on numbers you can trust.

What's the difference between this and a fractional CFO?

Same discipline, different framing: our fractional CFO page explains the engagement model — part-time executive leadership scoped to outcomes. If you're comparing against hiring in-house, start there.

The Local Picture

Built for How Miami Does Business

Miami is a market of momentum: international trade through the port and airport, a booming eCommerce and DTC scene, real estate that never sits still, and a founder culture that raises and deploys capital aggressively. That pace is an advantage — until the numbers fall behind it.

We see the same pattern across Miami engagements: revenue growing faster than reporting, inventory and receivables quietly eating the cash that growth produces, and funding conversations arriving before the financials are ready for them. Our job is to close that gap — clean books, real forecasts, and lender-grade financials — before it costs you a decision, a margin point, or a term sheet.

OCG Financial is founder-led by Oscar Cancino — an operator with a private equity background who's built, bought, and sold businesses. That experience, not a textbook, shapes every engagement.

What We See in the Chicago Market

Chicago businesses also tend to have long-standing banking relationships, which is an advantage right up until the moment financial reporting cannot support a larger facility or a new line of credit.

We build the system those decisions need: reconciled books, cost visibility at the level you actually operate, rolling cash forecasting, and statements a lender can underwrite without asking for three rounds of clarification.

Inside the Engagement

Not Hours. Outcomes.

Fractional doesn't mean diluted. Your engagement is scoped to a defined operating rhythm — the same one a great full-time CFO would run:

  • Monthly close & reporting — financials delivered on schedule, built on books we keep clean
  • Rolling 13-week cash forecast — refreshed continuously, so cash never surprises you
  • Margin & KPI review — the numbers that actually drive your model, watched monthly
  • Standing strategy session — where decisions get made, not just reported on
  • On-call judgment — when the unexpected lands mid-month, you have a CFO to call

Questions

Fractional CFO, Explained

What is a fractional CFO?

An experienced financial executive who works with your business part-time — giving you CFO-level forecasting, cash strategy, and reporting without the $250K+ cost of a full-time hire.

How many hours do I get?

Engagements are scoped to outcomes, not hours: a defined reporting and forecasting cadence, standing strategy sessions, and availability when decisions come up in between.

When does a business need a fractional CFO?

Usually when the numbers start driving real decisions — scaling past seven figures, margins getting harder to read, cash tightening as you grow, or lenders and investors entering the picture. Our guide covers the signals in depth.

What happens in the first 30 days?

Diagnostic, books verified (or cleanup started), core reporting live, and your first forward-looking forecast delivered — then the standing cadence begins.

Is this the same as your CFO services page?

Same discipline, two lenses: CFO services describes the work itself; this page describes the fractional engagement model most clients use to get it.

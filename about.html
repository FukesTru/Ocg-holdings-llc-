The Founder
"I want owners to understand their numbers, not just hand them off and hope somebody else is paying attention."

Oscar Cancino grew up in an entrepreneurial family, around businesses and the conversations that come with them. That was the education before the education: watching what it takes to build something, and what it costs when the numbers are not clear.

He went on to live it directly. Starting businesses. Buying them. Selling them. Winning some, losing others, and learning considerably more from the second category than the first.

Through all of it, the same gap kept appearing. There were plenty of professionals who could tell him what had already happened. Very few who could help him decide what to do next. The reporting always looked backward while every decision pointed forward.

OCG Financial is the firm he wishes he could have hired in those years.

The Philosophy

Clarity, Confidence, and Not Lying Awake About It

Ask most owners what they want from their financials and the honest answer is rarely a report. It is peace of mind. The confidence that comes from knowing where the business actually stands, what is coming, and that nothing is quietly going wrong in a corner nobody is watching.

That is the outcome this firm is organized around. Not deliverables for their own sake, but an owner who can walk into any decision, negotiation, or review knowing their numbers hold up.

It also means we would rather teach than gatekeep. Owners who understand their own financials make better decisions and ask sharper questions. That is a feature, not a threat, which is why the insight videos and written guides exist.

What We Believe

The Principles Behind Every Engagement

Operators Over Observers

Financial guidance should come from people who have met payroll and lived with the consequences of their own forecasts.

Systems Create Value

Businesses become more valuable when they run on process, infrastructure, and accountability rather than the owner's memory. Buyers pay for that difference.

Forward, Not Backward

Recording history is table stakes. The value is in what happens next: forecasts, scenarios, and decisions made while there is still time to act on them.

Teach, Don't Gatekeep

We would rather you understood your own numbers than depended on us to translate them every month.

One System, Not Four Vendors

Books, strategy, capital readiness, and tax work best sharing a single source of truth instead of scattered across providers who never speak.

Speed Is Part of the Product

Late numbers are nearly as unhelpful as wrong ones. Responsiveness and deadline discipline are commitments, not courtesies.

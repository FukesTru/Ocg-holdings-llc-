The Local Picture

What We See in the Miami Market

The recurring pattern in Miami engagements is revenue growing faster than visibility. Inventory and receivables quietly absorb the cash that growth produced, and margin erosion is discovered late because the books were weeks behind.

Our job is to close that gap before it costs a decision or a term sheet: clean books, honest margin data, a forward view of cash, and financials that hold up when someone serious reviews them.

The Local Picture

Built for How Jacksonville Does Business

Jacksonville businesses tend to be built on real operations: trucks, crews, inventory, contracts. Those are exactly the businesses where cash timing quietly decides everything — when progress payments land, how long receivables stretch, what the next equipment purchase does to liquidity.

That's the work OCG was built for: books that reflect reality, forecasts that respect cash timing, and reporting a lender can underwrite. Whether you're bidding bigger jobs, adding capacity, or preparing for an SBA loan, the numbers should be an asset — not the thing you're hoping nobody examines.

OCG Financial is founder-led by Oscar Cancino — an operator with a private equity background who's built, bought, and sold businesses. That experience, not a textbook, shapes every engagement.

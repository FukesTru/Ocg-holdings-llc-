The Local Picture

Built for How Los Angeles Does Business

Los Angeles is full of businesses that are exceptional at demand and improvising on finance. The brand looks incredible, the launch sells out, and nobody can say precisely what the contribution margin was after ad spend, returns, and fulfillment.

That's the gap OCG closes. We've served 30+ eCommerce and consumer brands, and the work is always the same at its core: know your true unit economics, know your cash position forward, and be ready when capital comes calling. LA's upside is real — this is how you keep it.

OCG Financial is founder-led by Oscar Cancino — an operator with a private equity background who's built, bought, and sold businesses. We work remote-first with clients across the country, which means Los Angeles businesses get senior attention without paying for local overhead.

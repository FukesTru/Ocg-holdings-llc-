The Local Picture

Built for How Tampa Does Business

Tampa's growth story is written in relocations and expansions: companies arriving for the market and the tax climate, founders scaling businesses that would have moved to bigger cities a decade ago. The opportunity is real — and so is the competition for capital, talent, and margin.

OCG works with Tampa Bay operators who want to grow on numbers, not narratives: books closed on schedule, margins tracked monthly, cash forecast weekly, and financials that hold up when a bank, investor, or acquirer starts asking questions.

OCG Financial is founder-led by Oscar Cancino — an operator with a private equity background who's built, bought, and sold businesses. That experience, not a textbook, shapes every engagement.

What does a typical Tampa engagement look like?

Most start with a readiness assessment, then bookkeeping cleanup if needed, then the monthly CFO cadence — reporting, forecasting, and a standing strategy session. The fractional CFO page walks through the first 30 days.

Yes — Tampa, St. Petersburg, Clearwater, and the wider Bay area. The engagement is remote-first, so geography inside the region doesn't change the service.

The Overview

Most Funding Applications Die in the Financials

Owners think funding fails on the pitch. It usually fails earlier — in books that don't reconcile, statements that contradict tax returns, and forecasts that read like wishes. Underwriters don't argue with you; they just pass.

Capital readiness means fixing that before it costs you the raise. We build financial packages that survive diligence: reconciled statements, defensible forecasts, clean entity records, and a capital strategy matched to what your business can actually service.

The Process

From "We Should Raise" to Funded

Capital readiness is a build, not a form. Here's the sequence.

01

Fundability Diagnostic

We assess your business the way an underwriter would: books, margins, debt capacity, and documentation. You get an honest read on what you'd qualify for today — and what's blocking more.

Week 1
02

Financial Cleanup & Structuring

Whatever the diagnostic surfaced gets fixed: books rebuilt, statements reconciled, entity records organized, and reporting brought to the standard capital providers expect.

Weeks 2–6
03

Capital Strategy

Bank debt, SBA, lines of credit, inventory or revenue-based financing, equity — we model what your cash flows can service and match the instrument to the goal, instead of taking the first term sheet that appears.

Weeks 4–8
04

Raise Support

Package assembly, lender questions, diligence responses, and negotiation support — through close. Then the same infrastructure keeps you covenant-compliant after the money lands.

Who It's For

Raise-Ready Is a Decision You Make Early

Strong Fit

Start Capital Readiness If…

  • You'll need growth capital in the next 6–18 months
  • You've been declined — or quoted rates that felt like a penalty
  • Inventory or receivables keep eating the cash growth produces
  • An acquisition, buyout, or expansion is on the table
  • Your financials have never been through real diligence
Sequence It

Handle These First If…

  • Books are significantly behind — cleanup comes first, and we'll sequence it
  • You're not sure you need capital at all — a strategy engagement answers that cheaper than a raise does
  • Margins are the real problem — CFO services fix the engine before you fuel it
Questions

Funding Support, Explained

Do you lend money directly?

No — we're not a lender. We make your business fundable: financials lenders trust, a clear capital strategy, and support through the raise process itself.

What kinds of funding do you help with?

Bank and SBA lending, lines of credit, revenue-based and inventory financing, and equity raises. The right instrument depends on your business — that's part of the strategy work.

How much capital has OCG supported?

OCG Financial has supported more than $35M in capital raises for its clients. See the case studies for what that looks like inside an engagement.

When should I start preparing to raise?

Before you need the money. Lenders fund businesses whose numbers they trust, and building that trust — clean books, consistent reporting, a credible forecast — takes months, not days.

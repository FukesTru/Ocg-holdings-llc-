The Approach

The Expensive Version of Tax Season Is the Rushed One

When records get rebuilt in March, two things follow. Legitimate deductions go undocumented because nobody can reconstruct what a transaction was for. And decisions that carried tax consequences already happened months earlier, without anyone running the numbers first.

Keeping records current through the year removes both problems. Your books stay reconciled monthly through our bookkeeping service, deadlines get tracked continuously, and preparation starts from data that was already right.

If you already work with a CPA you trust, keep them. Clean, organized, filing-ready records are the most useful thing a preparer can receive. We can also prepare returns directly, depending on how you want the engagement structured.

About OCG Financial

A Financial Firm Built by a Business Owner, Not an Accountant

OCG Financial exists because its founder needed it to exist — and couldn't find it anywhere. This is the firm Oscar Cancino wishes he'd been able to hire.

Oscar Cancino · Founder, OCG Financial
The Founder
"I'm not an accountant who learned to talk about business. I'm a business owner who built the financial infrastructure I wish I'd had, and now I build it for others."

Oscar is a business owner first. With a background in private equity, he's invested in, acquired, operated, and sold businesses across retail, e-commerce, and real estate. He's raised capital, navigated transactions, and made the kind of decisions that don't show up cleanly on a spreadsheet.

Along the way he kept running into the same problem: financial firms that could record the past but couldn't help him see the future. Books that were technically accurate and practically useless. Advisors who had studied business but never signed the front of a paycheck.

OCG Financial was built from that experience — the growing pains, the hard lessons, and the same mistakes he kept watching other founders repeat. When you work with OCG, you're not hiring someone who studied your problems from the outside. You're working with someone who's already lived through them.

The Journey

From Operator's Frustration to Financial Operating System

The Operator Years

Building, Buying, and Selling Businesses

Through a private equity background, Oscar invested in, acquired, operated, and sold companies across retail, e-commerce, and real estate — living the cash crunches, funding rounds, and margin battles his clients now face.

The Frustration

Great Accountants, No Answers

Every firm he hired could explain last quarter. None could tell him whether he could afford next quarter. The reporting looked backward; the decisions all pointed forward.

The Build

Designing the Missing Infrastructure

So he built it himself: clean books feeding real forecasts, cash-flow visibility feeding real decisions, financials structured the way lenders and investors actually read them.

Today

OCG Financial

That system now runs inside 30+ brands managing $84M in monthly revenue — delivered as bookkeeping, fractional CFO leadership, tax support, and funding readiness.

What We Believe

The Principles Behind Every Engagement

Operators Over Auditors

Financial advice should come from people who've made payroll, raised capital, and lived with the consequences of their own forecasts.

Forward, Not Backward

Recording what happened is table stakes. The value is in what happens next: forecasts, scenarios, and decisions made before the month ends.

Teach, Don't Gatekeep

We'd rather you understand your own numbers than depend on us to translate them. Educated clients make better decisions — and better partners.

One System, Not Four Vendors

Books, strategy, tax, and capital work best when they share one source of truth — not when they're scattered across four disconnected providers.

Speed Matters

Late numbers are almost as bad as wrong numbers. Responsiveness and deadline discipline are part of the product, not a courtesy.

Founder-Led, Always

You work with people who carry real accountability — not a rotating cast of junior staff reading from a template.

The OCG Philosophy

Profitable on Paper. Blind to Your Cash.

A P&L tells you what already happened. It won't tell you whether you can make payroll in six weeks. Growth creates cash pressure most owners don't see coming until it's already a problem. By then, the cost of not knowing has already compounded.

Common Questions

Before You Book, You Probably Want to Know…

What does OCG Financial actually do?

We're a founder-led financial partner. We combine bookkeeping, fractional CFO strategy, year-round tax support, and funding readiness into one integrated system — so your books, forecasts, taxes, and capital plans all work together.

How is OCG different from a traditional accounting firm?

Most firms are built by accountants. OCG was built by an operator — Oscar has invested in, acquired, operated, and sold businesses. We focus on the decisions in front of you, not just the records behind you. More on our story.

How quickly can we get started?

Engagements begin with a readiness assessment, and onboarding typically starts within days. Our 30-day CFO onboarding gets reporting, forecasting, and cash-flow visibility in place inside the first month.

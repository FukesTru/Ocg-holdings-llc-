The Overview

Your Accountant Tells You What Happened. This Tells You What to Do.

Almost every business we meet has some version of financial reporting. Almost none have a forward-looking financial plan — a model of how the business actually makes money, what the next twelve months look like under different scenarios, and where the breaking points are.

Strategy work builds that model and keeps it alive. It's the difference between "revenue is up, we're probably fine" and "we can fund the expansion from operations if DSO holds under 40 days — here's the trigger to watch."

It can stand alone as an advisory engagement, or sit inside a full fractional CFO relationship where we also run the reporting underneath it.

The Advisory Layer, Piece by Piece

Driver-Based Forecasting

A forward model built on your real drivers — orders, margins, payment terms, seasonality — not a spreadsheet that grows revenue 10% a year because someone typed it.

Scenario Planning

Best case, base case, and the case nobody likes to model. You'll know your breaking points before the market finds them for you.

Pricing & Margin Strategy

Unit economics that reveal which products, clients, or channels earn their keep — and pricing floors backed by real cost data.

Cash Flow Architecture

Payment terms, inventory cadence, and collection discipline designed as a system — the work behind our clients' 14-day average DSO improvement.

Standing Working Sessions

A recurring cadence where the model meets reality: decisions get made, assumptions get updated, and nothing drifts for a quarter unexamined.

An Operator's Judgment

Questions

Strategy & Advisory, Explained

How is financial strategy different from CFO services?

CFO services run your finance function end to end. Financial strategy is the advisory layer — forecasting, pricing, scenario planning, and decision support — and it can stand alone or sit inside a broader CFO engagement.

What does a typical engagement look like?

A deep diagnostic of your unit economics and cash dynamics, a forward model built around your real drivers, and a standing cadence of working sessions where decisions actually get made.

Can you help me decide whether to expand, hire, or raise?

That's exactly what this service is for — pressure-testing big moves against your cash reality before you commit, not after.

Do I need this if I already have an accountant?

An accountant tells you what happened. Strategy work tells you what to do next. Most of our clients have an accountant; almost none had a forward-looking financial plan before working with us. Start with our guide on outgrowing bookkeeping.

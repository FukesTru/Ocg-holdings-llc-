How Engagements Work

From Unclear Financials to Total Control

A deliberate sequence, because forecasts built on unreliable books are just expensive guesses.

01

Diagnose

We look at what is actually happening in the business, not only what the books say. Where the pressure is, where the margin is, and what the numbers are hiding.

02

Build the Foundation

Records get accurate, current, and organized. Historical cleanup is scoped up front so you know exactly what it involves before it starts.

03

Create Visibility

Reporting, forecasting, and cash flow clarity go live. From this point you can see forward instead of only backward.

04

Advise and Improve

We stay in it with you, supporting the decisions that come as the business grows, changes, and takes on new challenges.

Cash Flow Is the Whole Game

Profitable on Paper. Still Short on Cash.

A profit and loss statement tells you the business earned money. It will not tell you whether you can cover payroll in six weeks. Growth consumes cash before it produces cash, and most owners discover that gap only once it has become urgent.
